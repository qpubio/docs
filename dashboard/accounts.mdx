An **account** is the organization and billing context in the dashboard. Users sign in with email/password or OAuth (for example Google/GitHub).

## Sign-in

- Email and password signup/signin
- OAuth providers configured for the product
- Session refresh and sign-out from the dashboard UI

This authentication is **not** used for Socket/REST messaging. Messaging uses [API keys and JWTs](/core/auth).

## Invitations

Account members can invite others from the dashboard. Invitees accept or reject invitations while signed in.

## What to configure

- Profile details for your user
- Members and invitations for collaboration
- Billing / plan for the account (see [Plans](/pricing/plans))
