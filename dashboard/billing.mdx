The **Plans** page shows available QPub plans and your current subscription.

Open the dashboard and select **Plans** from the sidebar.

## Plan cards

Each plan (Free, Starter, Pro) is displayed as a card with its price, key limits, and support level. The current plan is highlighted.

## Upgrade or change plans

1. Click **Upgrade**, **Downgrade**, or **Switch** on a plan card.
2. For downgrades, confirm the change in the dialog.
3. You are redirected to a checkout page.
4. Stripe Checkout handles payment (free plans skip this step).
5. After success, the plan takes effect immediately or at the next billing period.

## Manage billing

The **Billing Management** section shows your current plan, billing period, and subscription status.

Click **Manage Billing** to open the Stripe Customer Portal — update payment methods, view invoices, and download receipts.

## Cancel subscription

Use **Cancel Subscription** in the billing section. The subscription remains active until the end of the current period.
