Understand the usage limits and quotas for different QPub plans.

## Free Plan Limits

- **API Requests**: Up to 10,000 requests per month
- **Connections**: Up to 100 concurrent connections
- **Data Transfer**: Up to 1GB per month
- **Channels**: Up to 10 channels

## Paid Plan Limits

### Starter Plan
- **API Requests**: Up to 100,000 requests per month
- **Connections**: Up to 1,000 concurrent connections
- **Data Transfer**: Up to 10GB per month
- **Channels**: Up to 100 channels

### Pro Plan
- **API Requests**: Up to 1,000,000 requests per month
- **Connections**: Up to 10,000 concurrent connections
- **Data Transfer**: Up to 100GB per month
- **Channels**: Unlimited channels

## Rate Limits

All plans are subject to rate limits to ensure fair usage and service stability.
