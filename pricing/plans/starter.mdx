The Starter plan is perfect for small applications and development projects that need more capacity than the free plan provides. This plan is designed for growing applications, small businesses, and teams that are ready to move beyond development and testing into production deployment.

## Starter Plan Features and Capabilities

The Starter plan provides significant capacity increases over the free plan, making it suitable for applications with moderate usage patterns and growing user bases. With 100,000 API requests per month, you have ten times the capacity of the free plan, providing ample room for growth and supporting applications with regular user activity.

Connection limits increase to 1,000 concurrent connections, supporting applications with many simultaneous users or complex architectures with multiple client types. This capacity is ideal for small to medium businesses, startups, and applications that need to support multiple users or devices simultaneously.

Data transfer increases to 10GB per month, providing substantial bandwidth for applications with regular file sharing, media content, or high-frequency data exchange. This limit supports most business applications while encouraging efficient data usage and providing room for growth.

Channel limits increase to 100 channels, providing significant flexibility for organizing complex applications with multiple features, user groups, or communication patterns. This capacity supports sophisticated application architectures and multi-tenant systems without the complexity of enterprise plans.

Email support provides direct access to QPub's support team for assistance with implementation, troubleshooting, and best practices. This level of support ensures that you can get help when you need it, making it easier to implement QPub successfully in your application.

Basic analytics provide usage insights and metrics to help you understand your application's usage patterns, optimize performance, and plan for future growth. These analytics help you make informed decisions about your QPub implementation and identify opportunities for optimization.

Standard integrations provide access to core integrations with popular platforms and services, making it easier to connect QPub with your existing tools and workflows. These integrations help streamline your development process and reduce the time required to implement QPub features.

## Pricing and Value

The Starter plan is priced at $19 per month or $190 per year (saving 17% with annual billing). This pricing provides excellent value for applications that need more capacity than the free plan while remaining affordable for small businesses and growing applications.

The monthly pricing provides flexibility for applications with variable usage patterns or teams that want to evaluate the plan before committing to annual billing. The annual pricing offers significant savings for applications with consistent usage patterns and teams that are committed to long-term QPub usage.

## Ideal Use Cases

The Starter plan is best suited for small applications that have outgrown the free plan's limits and need additional capacity for production use. This includes applications with regular user activity, moderate message volumes, and growing user bases that require more reliable service and support.

Development and testing teams benefit from the Starter plan's increased limits, which provide more room for testing different scenarios, user loads, and feature implementations. The additional capacity makes it easier to conduct thorough testing without worrying about hitting usage limits.

Growing projects that are transitioning from development to production find the Starter plan's features and support level appropriate for their needs. The plan provides the capacity and support necessary for successful production deployment while remaining cost-effective for smaller applications.

Teams getting started with QPub in production environments benefit from the Starter plan's balance of features, capacity, and support. The plan provides everything needed for successful implementation while offering room for growth as the application scales.

## Getting Started with the Starter Plan

Getting started with the Starter plan is straightforward and designed to minimize disruption to your existing QPub usage. Choose the Starter plan through your dashboard, and the upgrade takes effect immediately, providing access to all Starter plan features and increased limits.

Set up your first project to organize your QPub resources and take advantage of the increased channel limits. Projects help you separate different applications or environments and provide a foundation for managing your QPub usage as you grow.

Configure your channels to take advantage of the increased channel capacity. The Starter plan's 100-channel limit provides significant flexibility for organizing your application's communication patterns and supporting multiple features or user groups.

Start building your application with confidence, knowing that you have the capacity and support needed for successful production deployment. The Starter plan provides a solid foundation for growing applications while offering the flexibility to upgrade to higher plans as your needs evolve.
