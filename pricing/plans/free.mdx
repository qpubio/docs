Get started with QPub at no cost with our free plan. The free plan is designed to provide everything you need to learn, experiment, and build your first real-time applications without any financial commitment or credit card requirements.

## Free Plan Features

The free plan includes generous limits that allow you to explore QPub's capabilities and build functional applications. With 10,000 API requests per month, you have plenty of capacity for development, testing, and small-scale applications. This limit covers both REST API calls and WebSocket message publishing, giving you flexibility in how you use your quota.

Connection limits are set at 100 concurrent connections, which is sufficient for small applications, development environments, and testing scenarios. This allows you to connect multiple clients, test different user scenarios, and develop features that require multiple simultaneous connections.

Data transfer is limited to 1GB per month, providing adequate bandwidth for text-based messaging, small file transfers, and typical application data. This limit is generous enough for most development and small-scale production use cases while encouraging efficient data usage.

Channel creation is limited to 10 channels, which is perfect for organizing different features, user groups, or application components. This limit encourages thoughtful channel design and provides enough flexibility for most small to medium applications.

Community support provides access to our vibrant developer community, documentation, and community forums. While you won't have direct access to priority support channels, the community is active and helpful, and our comprehensive documentation covers most common scenarios.

## Plan Limitations

The free plan has some limitations compared to paid plans. There's no priority support, so you'll rely on community support and documentation for assistance. This is typical for free tiers and provides adequate support for learning and development.

Analytics are limited to basic metrics, providing essential information about usage but not the detailed insights available in paid plans. This limitation encourages upgrading as your application grows and requires more sophisticated monitoring.

Rate limits are set at standard levels, which are appropriate for most use cases but may be restrictive for high-frequency applications. These limits help ensure fair usage across all free plan users.

Support is community-only, which means you'll need to rely on community forums, documentation, and self-service resources for assistance. This approach keeps the free plan sustainable while still providing valuable support.

The free plan is perfect for learning and experimentation. Whether you're new to real-time messaging or exploring QPub's capabilities, the free plan provides everything you need to understand the platform and build your first applications.

Prototyping applications benefit from the free plan's generous limits and comprehensive feature set. You can build functional prototypes, test different approaches, and validate concepts without any cost commitment.

Small personal projects find the free plan's limits more than adequate. Whether you're building a personal chat application, experimenting with real-time features, or creating a small project for friends or family, the free plan provides all the resources you need.

Testing QPub features is made easy with the free plan. You can explore different APIs, test various configurations, and experiment with advanced features to understand how they work before committing to a paid plan.

## Getting Started with the Free Plan

Starting with the free plan is simple and straightforward. Sign up for a free account using your email address - no credit card required. The registration process takes just a few minutes and gives you immediate access to all free plan features.

Create your first project to organize your QPub resources. Projects help you separate different applications or environments and provide a foundation for managing your QPub usage as you grow.

Start with basic channels to understand how QPub's messaging system works. Create a few test channels, send some messages, and explore the real-time capabilities. This hands-on experience will help you understand how channels work and how to design your application's communication structure.

Explore QPub features by reading the documentation, trying different APIs, and experimenting with various configurations. The free plan gives you full access to all features, so you can learn and experiment without limitations.
