Choose a monthly plan that matches your connection and message volume. Change plans from the dashboard billing flow (Stripe).

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| [Free](/docs/pricing/plans/free) | $0 / month | Exploration and small prototypes |
| [Starter](/docs/pricing/plans/starter) | $25 / month | Growing production apps |
| [Pro](/docs/pricing/plans/pro) | $300 / month | Higher concurrency and throughput |

Compare numeric caps on [Limits](/docs/pricing/limits).
