Frequently asked questions about QPub pricing and billing.

## General Questions

### How does QPub pricing work?

QPub offers flexible pricing plans based on your usage needs, including free tiers and scalable paid plans.

### What's included in the free plan?

The free plan includes basic features with usage limits. Check our plans page for specific details.

### How is usage calculated?

Usage is calculated based on:
- API requests
- Data transfer
- Active connections
- Storage usage

## Billing Questions

### When will I be charged?

Charges are calculated monthly based on your actual usage.

### Can I change my plan anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time.
