Frequently asked questions about QPub pricing and billing. Understanding QPub's pricing model helps you make informed decisions about which plan is right for your application and how to optimize your usage for cost efficiency.

## General Pricing Questions

### How does QPub pricing work?

QPub offers flexible pricing plans designed to scale with your application's needs. The pricing model is based on usage rather than fixed monthly fees, ensuring you only pay for what you use. This approach makes QPub cost-effective for applications of all sizes, from small personal projects to large-scale enterprise systems.

The pricing structure includes a generous free plan for getting started, followed by scalable paid plans that increase capacity and features as your application grows. All plans include the same core features, with differences primarily in usage limits and support levels.

### What's included in the free plan?

The free plan includes all core QPub features with usage limits designed for development and small applications. This includes real-time messaging, channel management, authentication, and access to all APIs and SDKs. The free plan is perfect for learning, prototyping, and small-scale applications.

Free plan limits include 10,000 API requests per month, 100 concurrent connections, 1GB data transfer, and 10 channels. These limits are generous enough for most development and testing scenarios while encouraging efficient usage patterns.

### How is usage calculated?

Usage is calculated based on several factors that reflect your application's actual consumption of QPub resources. **API requests** include all REST API calls and WebSocket message publishing operations, providing a comprehensive measure of your application's interaction with QPub services.

**Data transfer** measures the actual bytes transmitted over the network, including message payloads, authentication data, and protocol overhead. This provides an accurate measure of bandwidth consumption.

**Active connections** count the number of simultaneous connections to QPub services, including WebSocket connections and persistent HTTP connections. This metric helps you understand your application's concurrent user capacity.

**Storage usage** includes message history, channel metadata, and user data stored by QPub on your behalf. This metric is typically minimal for most applications but can be significant for applications with extensive message history requirements.

## Billing and Account Management

### When will I be charged?

Charges are calculated monthly based on your actual usage during the billing period. The billing cycle begins when you upgrade to a paid plan and continues on a monthly basis. You'll receive a detailed invoice showing your usage breakdown and associated charges.

Usage is tracked in real-time, so you can monitor your consumption throughout the month and adjust your usage patterns if needed. The billing system provides detailed analytics to help you understand your usage patterns and optimize costs.

### Can I change my plan anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately and provide access to higher limits and additional features. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle to avoid interrupting your current usage.

Plan changes are designed to be seamless and don't require any changes to your application code. Your API keys and configuration remain the same, ensuring uninterrupted service during plan transitions.

### What happens if I exceed my plan limits?

If you exceed your plan limits, QPub will continue to serve your requests but may apply rate limiting to ensure fair usage. You'll receive notifications when approaching limits and can upgrade your plan to increase capacity.

For usage that significantly exceeds your plan limits, you may be contacted about upgrading to a higher plan or custom enterprise pricing. This approach ensures that all customers receive consistent service quality while maintaining fair usage across all plans.
