## How do I change plans?

Use the dashboard billing/checkout flow. Checkout is handled by Stripe.

## What am I billed for?

You subscribe to a monthly plan (Free, Starter, or Pro). Plans define connection, message, channel, and bandwidth limits. Monitor usage in the [dashboard](/dashboard/usage.mdx).

## What happens if I hit a limit?

Traffic may be rate limited or rejected (for example HTTP `429` on REST, or a rate-limit error on the Socket). Reduce usage or upgrade.

## Taxes

Checkout shows amounts in the dashboard. Confirm tax handling for your region at payment time.
