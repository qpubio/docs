## How do I change plans?

Use the dashboard billing/checkout flow. Plans are fulfilled through Stripe.

## What am I billed for?

You subscribe to a monthly plan (Free, Starter, or Pro). Plans define connection, message, channel, and bandwidth limits. Monitor usage in the [dashboard](/dashboard/usage).

## What happens if I hit a limit?

Requests or connections may be rate limited or rejected (for example HTTP `429` on REST, or protocol rate-limit errors). Reduce traffic or upgrade.
